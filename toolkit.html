Workflow explorer

Common business patterns, not live demos or claims about a deployed system. Each follows input → work → human boundary → output.

Customer request intake

  1. Input: An incoming request and an agreed routing checklist.
  2. Work: Extract requested details, flag missing information and draft a routing suggestion.
  3. Human boundary: A person checks sensitive details, chooses the owner and approves any customer reply.
  4. Output: A reviewed request with an owner and a draft response.
Read all patterns without using the explorer

Weekly operations reporting

  1. Input: Approved system exports and reporting definitions.
  2. Work: Reconcile records and draft a source-linked summary with exceptions.
  3. Human boundary: A person checks totals and exceptions before sharing or deciding.
  4. Output: A reviewed report, traceable sources and open questions.

Sales-to-delivery handoff

  1. Input: Approved scope, account notes and a delivery checklist.
  2. Work: Prepare a brief and identify missing commitments.
  3. Human boundary: Account and delivery owners confirm scope, dates and permissions before record changes.
  4. Output: An approved handoff brief with owners and open items.
