Engagements / Scope before spend

Know what you are buying before we build.

Start with discovery. Agree the deliverables, exclusions and price in writing. Our professional services rate is $150 per hour. Project fees follow the scope, not a guess made before we understand the problem.

Choose the level of commitment

Get a plan, keep advice close, commission a build, or support a system after delivery.

Readiness assessment

Short and fixed-fee, agreed before it starts. You receive a written AI assessment and a sequenced plan. For a build, you also get a specification, a price, and an explicit list of what is out of scope. If you take that document to another firm, that is a legitimate outcome.

Advisory retainer

A monthly allowance of hours at the published rate, for ongoing AI strategy, vendor evaluation and review. Month to month, cancellable with notice.

Build

Agentic workflows, AI-native CRMs, custom tools and integrations. Fixed-fee, quoted from the assessment or discovery document. Weekly working demos. A change to the scope is a written change to the price, agreed before the additional work begins.

Care plan

A monthly retainer after delivery: updates, monitoring of agent behaviour and model cost, a named contact, and a fixed allowance of change hours. Month to month, cancellable with notice.

What the quote covers

Our professional services rate is $150 per hour. Fixed fees are built from it, and any work outside an agreed scope is billed at it. Travel, third-party licences, cloud and AI model usage costs are passed through at cost with receipts.

Invoices are net 30 unless a signed order says otherwise. Work begins after a signed statement of work.

Discovery does not commit you to a build

The assessment is a useful deliverable in its own right. Use the written plan with your own team, take it to another firm, or decide not to proceed. If we build, the signed statement of work sets the scope and price before implementation starts.

When this is a bad fit

  • You need someone to start building on Monday without a written scope.
  • Nobody on your side can make a decision about the process being automated.
  • The cost of a build cannot be justified by the problem it would solve. We will say so.
  • You need AI agents explained from first principles before deciding whether you want them. We work with teams that already know what agentic systems are and want them done properly.
  • You want a guarantee of a financial result. Advice is our professional opinion; the business decisions, and their outcomes, stay yours.
