Subprocessors

Effective date: September 22, 2026

A subprocessor is a company we use that may process personal data on our behalf. This page lists them.

Seros is a solution development company and does not currently operate a hosted service. Where we build a system for you, it is normally deployed to infrastructure you own and control, and the vendors underneath it are yours, not ours, and are recorded in the Statement of Work rather than here. This page covers vendors that process personal data on our behalf — for our own business and for anything we host or operate for a client.

This list is Annex III to the Data Processing Addendum.

Current subprocessors

Seros currently operates a marketing website and no hosted customer-facing application, so no vendor processes client personal data on our behalf today. The website is statically hosted and has no analytics, no forms and no cookies beyond what the host sets to serve the files; enquiries arrive by ordinary email.

Add a row to the table below — and sign a DPA — before any vendor touches client personal data, including when we take on hosting or a care plan for a client system. Do not list a model that runs inside our own boundary as a third-party subprocessor; if a hosted AI provider is used on client data, it must be listed here first.

How customers are told about changes

Before adding a new subprocessor that will process Customer Personal Data, we give at least 30 days' notice to everyone subscribed to this list.

To subscribe: email team@seros.dev with the subject "Subprocessor notifications" and the address that should receive them. We keep the list separate from marketing email; there is nothing else in it. To unsubscribe, reply and say so.

A customer may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days of the notice. The objection process and its outcome are in Section 6 of the DPA.

Where a change is required urgently for security or continuity, we may make it first and notify promptly afterwards. The objection window then runs from the date of that notice.
