A useful first message

A few plain sentences are enough. These details help us decide whether we can help.

  1. The work you want AI to help with, in the words the people doing it would use.
  2. How it is handled today: the spreadsheet, the inbox, the person who remembers.
  3. Roughly how many people it affects, and how often.
  4. The systems it would have to touch.
  5. Any deadline that is real, and why it is real.

Keep the first email non-confidential. Do not send credentials or customer data. If the conversation needs it, we will sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement first.

What happens next

  1. We review the fit. We reply to project enquiries within two business days.
  2. We talk through the problem. If it looks like a fit, we book a free thirty-minute discovery call.
  3. We agree a next step. That may be a paid assessment or discovery sprint, or a clear answer that we are not the right fit.

The call is not a commitment to a project. See engagements and pricing for how written scope and fees are agreed before work begins.
