Book a scoping call.

Email us. A short, specific message gets a useful answer faster than a form ever would, and it means you are talking to the person who would do the work.

Email team@seros.dev →

What to put in the message

  1. The work you want AI to help with, in the words the people doing it would use.
  2. How it is handled today — the spreadsheet, the inbox, the person who remembers.
  3. Roughly how many people it affects, and how often.
  4. The systems it would have to touch.
  5. Any deadline that is real, and why it is real.

Do not send credentials, customer data, or anything confidential in a first email. If the conversation needs it, we will sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement first.

What happens next

  1. We reply to project enquiries within two business days.
  2. If it looks like a fit, we book a thirty-minute scoping call.
  3. If it is not a fit, we say so directly and, where we can, point you somewhere better.
